# FixPoint visual moodboard brief

## Direction

A calm, precise, service-oriented interface. Think modern repair operations software rather than a gaming storefront, a hardware benchmark site, or a generic fintech dashboard.

## Desired visual qualities

- Light neutral page background and clear white content surfaces.
- Deep graphite/navy typography.
- Restrained blue primary action colour.
- Muted blue-gray supporting text.
- Status colours reserved for actionable states.
- Thin borders, moderate radii and restrained shadows.
- Strong typographic hierarchy and generous spacing.
- Technical illustrations should be quiet and purposeful.

## Avoid

- Neon green/purple cyberpunk palettes.
- Excessive gradients and glass effects.
- Large decorative photos that do not explain a service.
- Fake ratings, testimonials, awards, certifications or repair guarantees.
- Overly dense tables on mobile.
- Icons that communicate status only by colour.

## Screens to collect

1. Repair-service landing page.
2. Service card grid and category filter.
3. Service detail and price estimate.
4. Customer repair tracker.
5. Repair intake form.
6. Technician queue and job detail.
7. Admin KPI and management table.
8. Mobile layout of the same core screens.

## Selection rule

A reference is useful only when it demonstrates a clear pattern that FixPoint needs. One strong, well-documented reference per screen is better than a folder full of random screenshots.
